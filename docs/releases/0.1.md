RoboSeq 0.1 is the first release of [RoboSeq](https://github.com/joakimskurk/fm1roboseq) for the M-VAVE FM-1. In
RoboSeq two robots walk a 5 x 5 board of belts, rotators and zaps, and their moves are the music. It began as a
browser instrument and runs here as the FM-1's own firmware, on [Dinghy](https://github.com/zvenson/dinghy) 0.4.

**It has not run on a real FM-1 yet.** That is why this is a pre-release. It is tested on a computer and in
[fm1-emu](https://github.com/joakimeriksson/fm1-emu):
- the game matches the browser original tick by tick;
- every note of 36 scenarios matches, and most voices are within 0.15 dB of the HTML's;
- the emulator's strict mode passes: the worst audio block takes 31 % of its budget, and the saves work.

If you have an FM-1, **[FIRST_FLASH.md](https://github.com/joakimskurk/fm1roboseq/blob/main/FIRST_FLASH.md)** is a
half-hour checklist with the way back. A report from it is what moves RoboSeq onto the Firmware Hub.

## What is in it

- **HOME** is the board. PLAY starts the robots. The white keys turn a robot's steps, and the black keys nudge a robot
  off its program for a step: turn, move, dash or ping, and a swap of their programs.
- **The pages:**
  - SEQ, the robots' programs as step cards;
  - SCL, Voice Mod (the scale and root, step by step) and Seq 2 (the song: boards, drums, progressions);
  - LFO, the drums;
  - FX, the mix;
  - ENV, the boards and a board builder;
  - GLO, tempo, speed and the loop's length.
- **BATTLE** (GLO, white key 16): two players, a robot each, fighting on the beat.
- **REC** turns the keys into a SID-like lead to play over the robots, and ARP arpeggiates it.
- **The project is kept** over a power-off. It saves only while stopped, and SAVE is lit until it has.
- The version is shown in the splash's corner: `v0.1`.

All the controls are in [CONTROLS.md](https://github.com/joakimskurk/fm1roboseq/blob/main/docs/roboseq/CONTROLS.md),
and the manual is the [README](https://github.com/joakimskurk/fm1roboseq#readme).

## The package

| | |
|---|---|
| File | `roboseq-0.1.fwsc`, with `SHA256SUMS` beside it |
| SHA-256 | `eb6c4e503227cb86e84953dcb8fc1d7dbc774bd2e102828b578be6f02b436e80` |
| Identity | `FM-1_965` (the same for every RoboSeq build) |
| Installs on | M-VAVE's V15 or V16, or any firmware with the FM-1 update loader (every Hub firmware) |
| Flash chips | both: the Puya P25Q80 and the UC25HQ80 of FM-1s sold since autumn 2026 |
| Install | `python3 tools/fm1_install.py roboseq-0.1.fwsc` |
| The way back | hold **OCT-** while you switch on: the USB rescue, from where any firmware installs again |

RoboSeq keeps its project in flash sectors of its own. It does not read or write other firmwares' projects or
samples.
